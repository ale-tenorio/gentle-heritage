# Gentle Heritage。— Domain Glossary

## Brand

**Gentle Heritage。** — The brand name. The `。` (Japanese full stop) is intentional and permanent. Appears in nav, footer, and browser tab title.

**@gentleheritage** — The Instagram handle. Primary contact channel for the shop. Used in the footer and contact page.

## Shop concepts

**The Edit** — The full product catalog. Used as the shop page heading.

**Pre-loved** — Secondhand/used items. Displayed as a badge on product cards and in filters. Distinct from **New** items.

**Fit note** — A short sizing annotation per product describing how a piece fits on a shorter or petite frame. Shown in the product modal.

**Vision Board** — The wishlist feature. Displays saved items as pinned cutout cards on a kraft-paper board.

**Your Bag** — The cart. Accessible via the bag icon in the nav.

## Inventory

**Stock** — Placeholder inventory only (12 items). Not real products yet. Will be replaced with actual inventory.

**Stock photos** — Unsplash/Pexels images to replace gradient placeholders. Not yet added.

## Pricing & currency

**Currency** — USD ($). All prices displayed with `$` symbol.

## Policy

**Return policy** — TBD. Not finalised.

## Checkout

**Checkout** — Flow TBD. User is considering Venmo and PayPal as payment options.

## Visual theme

**Three variations** — Editorial (Bodoni Moda), Warm (DM Serif Display, current default), Graphic (Raleway). Final direction not yet decided — theme switcher stays in place.
